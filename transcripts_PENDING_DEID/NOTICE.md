# Transcripts withheld pending de-identification

The 1,100 full conversation transcripts are **intentionally not published
here**.

Although the conversations are synthetic (produced by a language model
simulating the patient), each is seeded from a real, de-identified
chief-complaint text extracted from routine records. That source text can
contain **incidental identifiers** (e.g., third-party names such as a
referring physician, referral origin, free-text notes). Publishing the
transcripts as-is would risk leaking patient- or third-party-identifiable
information.

Before any transcript release, the following is required:
1. An automated PII scrub of the chief-complaint-derived content
   (names, dates, locations, document/record numbers, referral sources).
2. **Human review** of a sample by a clinical team member.
3. Institutional sign-off on the de-identified set.

A de-identified release of the transcripts is planned as **future work**,
once this process is complete.
